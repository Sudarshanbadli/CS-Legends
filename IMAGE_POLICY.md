# Image and portrait licensing policy

CS Legends does not assume that a historical portrait is free to reuse. Copyright can remain in a photograph even when the subject lived long ago.

## Acceptable image sources
- Wikimedia Commons files explicitly marked Public Domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA, or another licence approved by maintainers.
- Institutional archives with explicit reuse terms.
- Government/public archives whose terms permit redistribution.

## Required credit fields
Subject, image title, creator, source file page, licence name/version, attribution text, changes, date checked.

## Not acceptable
- Search-engine thumbnails without a file page.
- Images marked “all rights reserved” unless written permission is obtained.
- Images whose creator or licence cannot be established.
- AI-generated portraits presented as historical photographs.

A blank portrait is better than an unlicensed portrait.
