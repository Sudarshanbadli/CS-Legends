OUR PURPOSE

History deserves
good citations.

CS Legends is an educational project documenting the people behind ideas, inventions and institutions. We distinguish verified contributions from popular labels, acknowledge collaboration, and record portrait licences rather than copying images without permission.

Read editorial policy ↗
01

Evidence first

Prefer institutional archives, original papers, award citations and oral histories.

02

Credit the creator

Every portrait must have a verified licence and a complete attribution record.

03

History is collaborative

Breakthroughs are rarely the work of one person. We make context visible.
