# Research starting points

- IEEE Computer Society Computer Pioneers: https://history.computer.org/pioneers/index.html
- ACM A.M. Turing Award biographies: https://amturing.acm.org/
- Computer History Museum: https://www.computerhistory.org/
- ACM History Committee oral histories: https://history.acm.org/projects-and-initiatives/oral-histories/
- Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/ (image licence must be checked per file)
- MacTutor History of Mathematics: https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: https://plato.stanford.edu/
- National Academies: https://www.nationalacademies.org/
- NASA History: https://history.nasa.gov/

These are discovery and verification sources. Link directly to the relevant individual biography, award citation, paper, archive record, or oral history in each completed profile.
